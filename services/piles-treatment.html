Overview

What piles are, and why homeopathy works.

Piles (haemorrhoids) are inflamed or prolapsed rectal veins. Conventional treatment usually targets the local swelling with creams, banding, or surgery. Homeopathy looks further: at venous tone, bowel habits, portal circulation, diet, posture, and the patient's overall susceptibility to congestion and inflammation.

When the right constitutional remedy is selected, the swelling resolves, the pain settles, and — crucially — the underlying tendency toward recurrence is addressed. That is why classical homeopathy succeeds where short-term local treatment fails.
