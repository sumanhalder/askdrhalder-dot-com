Overview

Diabetes is a metabolic story, not a sugar number.

HbA1c is the report card — but the lesson is in the metabolism. Insulin resistance, pancreatic fatigue, hepatic glucose handling, stress hormones, sleep, gut microbiome, and constitutional susceptibility are all part of the picture. Treating only the sugar number never resolves the underlying story.

Classical homeopathy addresses the patient's full metabolic and constitutional picture. Combined with clear dietary, sleep, and movement guidance, this often leads to better HbA1c, more stable energy, fewer cravings, and — over time — reduced medication load under supervision.
