Overview

Stones are a metabolic signature.

Kidney stones recur because the metabolic tendency that produces them hasn't been addressed. Lithotripsy breaks the stone; it doesn't change the urine, the diet, or the constitutional susceptibility to forming new ones.

Classical homeopathy looks at the full picture: stone type (oxalate, uric acid, phosphate), urinary pH pattern, hydration, dietary triggers, digestion, stress, and the patient's overall constitutional picture. The right remedy shifts the underlying metabolism — and stones stop coming back.

What outcomes to expect.

Often pass within 4–8 weeks of starting treatment. Pain episodes reduce in frequency and intensity.

Medium stones (6–12 mm)

May take 3–6 months to dissolve or pass. Surgical intervention can usually be avoided with consistent care.
