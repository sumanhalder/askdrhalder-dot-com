FAQ

Common questions.

If you have a question that isn't answered here, please call us — we'll be happy to help.

How long is the first consultation?

Plan for 45 minutes. It's a detailed constitutional case-taking — Dr. Halder will ask about your main concern, but also about sleep, digestion, energy, food, mood, and medical history. The depth of this first session is what makes the remedy selection accurate.

Do I need to stop my current medication?

No. Please continue any prescribed medication unless your prescribing physician advises otherwise. Homeopathic remedies have no chemical interactions with conventional drugs and work alongside them.

How many follow-ups will I need?

Typically every 3–6 weeks during active treatment, depending on the condition. Most patients see meaningful improvement within 2–3 months, with full constitutional work taking 6–18 months.

Do you offer online consultations?

Yes — for follow-ups especially, and for first consultations when travel isn't possible. Online case-taking is as detailed as in-clinic, conducted over video call.

What does treatment cost?

Consultation fees and remedy costs are reasonable and discussed upfront. Please call the clinic for current fee details. Remedy costs are typically modest and included in follow-up pricing.

Is homeopathy safe for children and during pregnancy?

Yes. Homeopathic remedies have no chemical side-effects and are widely used in paediatric and obstetric care. They're safe for all ages, including infants and during pregnancy.

What if my condition isn't listed on your website?

We treat most chronic and recurring conditions homeopathically. If you're unsure whether your case is suitable, call us — we'll be honest about whether we can help or whether you need a different kind of care.

How quickly can I get an appointment?

Same-week slots are usually available. For urgent cases, please mention it when you call and we'll do our best to fit you in.
