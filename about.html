Biography

Dr. Suman Halder, BHMS, MD (Hom.)

Dr. Suman Halder is a Kolkata-based classical homeopathic physician and the founder of AskDrHalder.com. Trained at one of India's premier homeopathic institutions, he went on to specialise in chronic and complex cases that often arrive after conventional medicine has reached its limits.

Over fifteen years of clinical practice have shaped a philosophy that is rare in modern medicine: treat the patient, not the diagnosis. Every consultation begins with a 45-minute case-taking where Dr. Halder listens — to the chief complaint, yes, but also to sleep, digestion, mood, weather-sensitivity, food preferences, and the small details that classical homeopathy treats as essential.

"The remedy is already written in the patient's story. My job is to read it carefully enough."

His results speak in numbers — a 95% success rate across the conditions he treats, more than 8,000 patients, and a 4.9-star Google rating built entirely on verified word-of-mouth. But the metric Dr. Halder tracks most carefully is simpler: how many patients no longer need him.

Philosophy

What we believe — and how we practice it.

Four principles guide every consultation at AskDrHalder.

Time is part of the cure

A 45-minute first consultation isn't a luxury — it's the minimum required to understand the case deeply enough to prescribe correctly.

One remedy, well chosen

Classical homeopathy works through one constitutional remedy at a time. We don't combine, stack, or shotgun. The right single remedy does the work.

Treat the whole person

Two patients with the same diagnosis often need different remedies. We treat the constitution, not the label.

Honest about prognosis

If homeopathy isn't the right tool for your case, we'll tell you. If it is, we'll tell you how long to expect before real change.

Safe, gentle, non-toxic

Homeopathic remedies have no chemical side-effects and no interactions with conventional medicine. Suitable for all ages.

Built for the long term

We're not optimising for short-term suppression. The goal is constitutional strengthening — fewer relapses, less medication, lasting vitality.

Vision & Mission

To be a beacon of hope and healing.

We exist to restore health and vitality through personalised, classical homeopathic care — and to do it in a way that respects the patient's time, intelligence, and individuality. Our work is measured not in prescriptions written, but in patients who no longer need us.
